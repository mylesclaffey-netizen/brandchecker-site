Does AI know your pricing?

Buyers ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity what your software costs — and those answers are often out of date or wrong. Check what they say against your own pricing page.

Step 1 of 2 · your pricing

We read your public pricing page and write down the prices, plans and free-trial terms it states. You check and correct them before anything is judged.

Step 2 of 2 · check and ask

This is the answer key. Name the plan on each line (“Pro plan costs…”) so a price can be matched to the right plan. Fix anything the reading got wrong or missed — a wrong line gives wrong verdicts.

Where each line came from
    What happens next: we ask four AI models three buyer questions about your pricing (12 answers), then judge every price, plan and trial claim as true, false or unverified (your lines don’t cover it). It takes about 20 seconds.
