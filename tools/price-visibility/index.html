Can AI crawlers see your prices?

A buyer who asks an AI “how much does it cost?” gets an answer built from what AI crawlers could read on your pricing page. Many pricing pages draw their numbers with JavaScript — and a crawler that reads only the HTML your server sends sees plans with no prices.

We fetch the page as a browser and as Googlebot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot, and read the prices in the HTML each one gets. Takes a few seconds, no sign-up.
