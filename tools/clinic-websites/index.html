Clinic website check

Finds clinics across Prague on Google Maps and shows which of them have no website on their Google profile, or only a Facebook page or a booking listing. The clinics with the most reviews and no website are the best people to talk to.

It starts in the centre of Prague and works outwards, and stops as soon as it has the number you picked. Opening hours, photos and the profile's other details are not read, only what Google shows publicly.
