Total Accessible Search Market

How much search demand exists in an industry, and how much of it a site could realistically win. Three layers: the total market (monthly searches, with close variants counted once), the accessible market (clicks winnable at an achievable position, for a brand-new entrant and for a specific site's authority), and what a site captures today.
