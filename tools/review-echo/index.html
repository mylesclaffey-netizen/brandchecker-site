Free tool · for B2B SaaS

Negative-review echo

A buyer asks an AI “what are the cons of your product?” and gets a short list that stands in for a hundred reviews. Whatever the models repeat — true or not — is what the next buyer reads first. See what they say, which complaints they all agree on, and what to publish in answer.

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini answer from memory, Perplexity searches the web; each is asked two ways. Takes about 20 seconds.
