B2B SaaS diagnostic

What does AI tell software buyers about your product? One run checks your pricing, your place on competitors’ “alternatives” lists, your review-site profiles and your integrations — and puts the fixes in order.

Step 1 of 2 · your product

We read your pricing page, your integrations and who you compete with, and fill in the three answer keys below for you to correct.

Step 2 of 2 · check the answer keys

Your pricing

One fact per line, naming the plan: “Pro plan costs $49 per seat per month billed annually.”

Your competitors

Up to 5, one per line — a name, and optionally a comma and its website.

Your integrations

Integrations you really have, up to 8, one per line.

What happens next: four checks run at once — about 40 seconds and roughly 30 AI and search calls. Each also exists as its own tool if you want to run one alone. You can leave any section empty to skip that check.
