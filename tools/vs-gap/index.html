Free tool · for B2B SaaS

Vs & alternatives gap finder

Software buyers shortlist by asking an AI for “alternatives to” your competitor. If it doesn’t name you, you’re not on the list. Find out where — and whether you have the comparison pages to change it.

Step 1 of 2 · you and your competitors

We ask Perplexity, which searches the web, who buyers compare you with. You edit the list next.

Step 2 of 2 · competitors to check

Write the product name, and optionally a comma and its website. Pick the products you most often lose deals to — or that buyers most often compare you with.

What happens next: for each competitor we ask ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity for its best alternatives, ask Perplexity to compare you with it, and look on your site for a comparison page and an alternatives page. It takes about 30 seconds.
