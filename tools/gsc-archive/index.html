Search Console Archive

Google keeps your Search Console data for 16 months, then each day is deleted. Connect a property and we save the days that are about to disappear — the oldest five weeks Google still has, then another week every Monday — with daily totals, the top queries and pages of each month, and every query-by-page row. Your last 16 months stay in Search Console; this keeps what comes after.

Add a property

Properties you have verified in Search Console. Adding one saves the five weeks of data Google is about to delete, then saves the next week every Monday.

What gets stored

For each day we save, exact clicks, impressions, click-through rate and average position, plus every query-by-page row Search Console returns (up to 250,000 a day). We save a day when it is among the oldest five weeks Google still has, and each Monday we check the next week, so every day gets five chances before Google drops it. That means the archive starts with data about 15 months old and grows from there — for anything newer, use Search Console itself. Google anonymises rare queries, so the rows add up to slightly less than the exact total; the daily totals include them. The archive is private to your account, and “Stop and delete” removes every stored file and row. Free while in beta.
