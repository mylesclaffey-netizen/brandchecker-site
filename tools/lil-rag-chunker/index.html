How to chunk

Medium suits most chatbots. Small for short factual answers, large for long-form reading.

Prefixes every chunk with “Page title › Section”, so it still makes sense when retrieved on its own.

When a long section is split, the last paragraph of one chunk is repeated at the start of the next.

Uses each page’s structured data: its types and key facts as metadata, FAQ markup as one Q&A chunk per question, and a flag where the markup and the page disagree.

Re-reads pages that are near-empty without JavaScript in a real browser. Needs an access code.

Nothing is stored: the chunks come back to your browser and the ZIP is built here.
