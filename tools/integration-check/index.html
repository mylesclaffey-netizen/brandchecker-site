Does AI know your integrations?

“Does it integrate with HubSpot?” is one of the first things a software buyer asks an AI. If the answer is no, or a shrug, you lose the deal the integration would have won. Check what AI says — and whether you have a page that could tell it.

Step 1 of 2 · your integrations

We read your sitemap and integrations page for the integrations you list. You choose which to check next.

Step 2 of 2 · what to check

These are the answer key: AI is asked about each one and a “no” counts as wrong. Pick the integrations your buyers ask about most.

What happens next: ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity are asked, for each integration, whether your product has it — and what your product integrates with, unprompted. We also look on your site for a page for each integration. It takes about 20 seconds.
