Competitor Publishing Tracker

Name the competitors you watch, and add your own site beside them. We read their sitemaps every week and show you everything they publish, week by week. Add topics if you also want to see who covers the subjects you care about.

New pages are found by comparing a competitor's sitemap with the week before, so the first look only records what is there (and shows the pages the sitemap says changed most recently). A sitemap lists additions, not edits to old pages, and a site without a usable sitemap cannot be watched. Tag, category, author and page-number listings are kept apart from real pages. Each new page is opened once to read its title, description and date; nothing else is stored from it.
