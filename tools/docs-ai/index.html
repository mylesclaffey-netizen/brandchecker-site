Docs AI-readiness check

Product docs are what AI assistants lean on to answer “how do I do X in your product?” and “does it support Y?”. If AI crawlers can’t fetch them, can’t read them without JavaScript, or can’t tell how current they are, the answer comes from guesswork or someone else’s blog post.

We find your docs from your homepage’s links, then check robots.txt, llms.txt, markdown, the text in the HTML of pages from different sections, and how recently the docs changed. Takes about 10 seconds, no sign-up.
