Cluster Activity Tracker

Follow a group of keywords week by week and see what happened around each move: the changes you made, Google updates, new AI models, and pages your competitors published on the same topic.

Visibility is your clicks from Search Console for the cluster's queries, or, for a rank-checked cluster, the estimated clicks of its weekly checks (search volume times the click rate at the position). The numbers beside each event compare the four complete weeks before it with the four after: they show what moved around it, not what caused it. Several things usually change at once, a rollout takes weeks, and small numbers move for no reason, so treat a link between an event and a move as a lead to check. Google updates come from Google's own Search Status Dashboard; model releases come from OpenRouter's public list and cover the main families only.
