Does AI recommend your app?

People now ask ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity which app to download. Ask them the questions your future users ask, and see whether they recommend your app, where it ranks, and who they recommend instead.

Step 1 of 2 · your app

The country sets which App Store we read and where the assistants are told you are.

Step 2 of 2 · the questions

Leave your app’s name out: a question that names it gets dropped, because it would rig the answer.

What happens next: ChatGPT and Claude answer from what they already know, and Perplexity searches the web, for every question (up to 15 answers). Each lists the apps it recommends; we check where yours is. About 30 seconds.
