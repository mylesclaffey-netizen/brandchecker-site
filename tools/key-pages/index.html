Key-pages audit

A software buyer’s decision runs through a handful of pages. This finds each one on your site — homepage, pricing, product, integrations, a comparison page, customers, docs and security — and checks them all at once, so you see which of the pages that decide a deal have problems.

We find the pages from your homepage’s links, your sitemap and the usual addresses, then run the page audit’s checks on each. Takes about 10 seconds, no sign-up.
