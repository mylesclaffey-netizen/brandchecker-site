Is your pricing localised?

A buyer in London asking an AI what your software costs should hear pounds; one in Berlin, euros. AI crawlers mostly fetch from the United States and read the default page — so if every market sees one price list, or your UK page shows dollars, that is the price AI repeats.

We read your pricing page’s hreflang tags, look for a page per market, and read the currency each one shows. Takes about 15 seconds, no sign-up.
